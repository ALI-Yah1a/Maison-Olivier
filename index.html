OUR STORY

Forty years of quiet,
stubborn cooking.

Maison Olivier opened on a rainy Thursday in 1984 with eight tables, one stove, and a chef who refused to print a permanent menu. Four decades later not much has changed — except the queue at the door.

We work with twenty-three small producers within a hundred kilometers of the city. Our bread is baked in-house each afternoon, our butter is churned every Friday, and the wine list is built almost entirely from low-intervention growers we visit ourselves.
